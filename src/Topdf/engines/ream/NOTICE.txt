TOPDF modification, 2026-11-02:
excel/print-model.js shrinkToFit used only the first column width for merged cells.
The patched calculation sums the visible columns through the merged cell's end,
bounded by the current print window, and excludes hidden columns.
Unmerged cell behavior remains the original single-column calculation.
